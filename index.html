Picks

Pick Over or Under on all 30 win totals. Each correct pick is worth 1 point.

Locks

Choose 5 picks as locks. A correct lock is worth 3 points total instead of 1. A wrong lock is worth 0.

Deadline

Picks lock 10 minutes before the season's first tip-off. Until then each person sees only their own sheet; everyone's picks appear at the deadline.

Projected points

Until the season ends, each team's result is projected from its current win % against the line's win % (line ÷ 82). A team with no games played isn't scored yet.

Chase

A team is live while both outcomes are still possible. Once a team's wins pass the line the Over is clinched; once it can't reach the line even by winning out, the Under is clinched. Clinched results override pace.
